Pot Rules

  1. Winner-take-all pot. Whoever of the 7 participating teams finishes highest in the final league standings/playoffs takes the entire pot — regardless of whether they're the league's overall champion.
  2. Buy-in. Each of the 7 participants pledges $250 mxn to the pot.
  3. Payout. Within 5 business days of the winner being announced, each participant pays their $250 mxn directly to the winner, or deposits it with Potatoe (treasurer) — who will forward any funds he's collected to the winner within that same 5-business-day window.
